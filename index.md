## The 5 steps (each has its own page)

<div class="card step"><div class="stepnum">1</div><div>
<h3><a href="guide/step1/">Step 1 — Name Your One Service</a></h3>
<p class="faint">Pick the single thing whose uptime matters most (the "which door do I guard" question). 10 minutes.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="card step"><div class="stepnum">2</div><div>
<h3><a href="guide/step2/">Step 2 — Write Your First SLO</a></h3>
<p class="faint">One number you can defend: "99.9% of the time, X happens in Y seconds." No math above arithmetic. 15 minutes.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="card step"><div class="stepnum">3</div><div>
<h3><a href="guide/step3/">Step 3 — Measure the Real Thing</a></h3>
<p class="faint">Put one honest sensor on the system so you're measuring reality, not your hopes. 20 minutes + a lunch.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="card step"><div class="stepnum">4</div><div>
<h3><a href="guide/step4/">Step 4 — Write One Runbook</a></h3>
<p class="faint">The 6-line document that turns "I panic" into "I follow the card." 15 minutes.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="card step"><div class="stepnum">5</div><div>
<h3><a href="guide/step5/">Step 5 — Do Your First Blameless Review</a></h3>
<p class="faint">Look back on the week without blaming anyone (including yourself), and write one next-action. 15 minutes.</p>
</div></div>

<p><a href="guide/playground/"><i class="fa-solid fa-flask"></i> Optional but wonderful: the Playground — a fake service that can't break a thing, to rehearse on</a> · <a href="guide/glossary-lite/">No-jargon Word Box</a></p>

<div class="callout good">
<span class="faint"><i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i> The one idea that keeps you going</span>
<p><b>Reliability is a number you choose, looked at honestly — not a personality trait you're born with.</b> The whole field is just: pick a number, measure it, notice when it's wrong, and make the next thing better without drama. That's it. The AI teammate simply makes the "measure it" and "notice when it's wrong" parts faster, so you can spend your energy on the "make it better" part.<b></p>
</div>
